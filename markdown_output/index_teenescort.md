# **Teen Escort Service**(1994-??)

---

## **History and Background Information**

Teen Escort Service was a teen transport company which operated as an affiliate of the [WWASP](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/wwasp) organization. WWASP officials say Teen Escort is not affiliated with their organization, but was one of three approved transport services that WWASP recommended to parents. The address affiliated with the organization was [55 E 200 S, La Verkin, UT 84745](https://www.google.com/maps/place/55+E+200+S,+La+Verkin,+UT+84745/@37.1976877,-113.2683136,74m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x80caf15c6ca14027:0x3c927618c53d4dfe!8m2!3d37.1977358!4d-113.2681272) .
