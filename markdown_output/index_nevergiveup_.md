# **Never Give Up Youth Healing Center**(2019-2023) Amargosa Valley, NV

*Residential Treatment Center*

---

## **History & Background Information**

Never Give Up Youth Healing Center (also called Never Give Up Behavioral Health Services) is a [WWASP](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/wwasp) -affiliated beahvior-modification program founded in 2019. The program is marketed as an inpatient psychiatiatric residential treatment facility which houses children aged 8-17 dealing with "moderate to severe emotional challenges including youth with bipolar disorders, depression, psychosis, self-harm, post traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), attention deficit, and attachment disorders." The program is co-ed, although survivors report that the genders are kept completely seperate.

The program is located at [3020 South, NV-373, Amargosa Valley, NV 89020](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Never+Give+Up+Youth+Healing+Center/@36.4959159,-116.4240729,302m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x8b4c53efc3f7fdf7!8m2!3d36.4956939!4d-116.422475) , which is the former location of two other WWASP programs, [Horizon Academy](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/horizonacademy) and [Northwest Academy](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/northwestacademy) . It is believed that Never Give Up Youth Healing Center is is the reincarnation of Northwest Academy, which was closed in January of 2019 shortly before Never Give Up opened.

Never Give Up closed in 2023.

## **Abuse Allegations**

In December of 2019, the Nye County Sherriff's Department launched an [investigation](https://www.fox5vegas.com/news/local/allegations-of-physical-sexual-abuse-made-against-youth-center-in-amargosa-valley/article_e997d500-16a1-11ea-8845-436e84ebe6ed.html) into the facility following allegations of sexual and physical abuse, as well as the staff not reporting the claims within the mandated 24 hours. It is reported that the victim, who was 11 at the time, had been repeatedly sexually assaulted beginning in July 2019 by another resident at the facility, who was 16 at the time. The victim also reported that in September of 2019, they were physically abused by a staff member at the facility, which led to that staff member's termination. One staff member was arrested for obstructing a public officer while the authorities were executing the search warrant in December. In 2020, the victim and their family filed a lawsuit seeking over $30,000 in damages.
