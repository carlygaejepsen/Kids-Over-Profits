# **Allegany Boys Camp**(2011-present) Oldstown, MD

*Christian Wilderness Camp*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Allegany Boys Camp is a [WRTCA](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/wrtca) behavior modification program that opened in 2011. It is marketed as a year-round Christian residential wilderness camp for boys (9-15) who are struggling with a variety of behavioral or emotional challenges.

Allegany Boys Camp began as a branch of the [Bald Eagles Boys Camp](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/baldeaglesboys) in Mill Hall, PA.
