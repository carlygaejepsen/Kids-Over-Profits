# **CEDU Middle School**(1991-2005) Running Springs, CA

*Emotional Growth Boarding School*

---

## **History and Background Information**

CEDU Middle School opened by Mel Wasserman in 1991 on the same campus as [CEDU High School](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/ceduhs) at [3500 Seymour Rd, Running Springs, CA 92382](https://www.google.com/maps/place/3500+Seymour+Rd,+Running+Springs,+CA+92382/@34.1909928,-117.0959064,200m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x80c4ab991a1c3cd5:0x8b26b778e90134a4!8m2!3d34.1906664!4d-117.0954524) . CEDU Middle School used the same program as CEDU High School, but it was targeted towards Middle Schoolers. CEDU Middle School took children as young as nine years old. In 1996, the tuition was [reported](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OhFuppZRguTBnbBnRsauG7qz1wtUnFOz/view) to be $4,150 per month ($7,604 adjusted for 2022 inflation). The program also required additional enrollment fees totaling $2,150 ($3,939 adjusted for 2022 inflation).

Mel Wasserman was a member of the notorious cult [Synanon](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/synanon) . He used to sponsor children who were in Synanon. He reportedly left Synanon after expressing great interest in its methods, and sought to start CEDU as a "watered-down" version targeted towards kids. Wasserman based CEDU's program heavily on the program of Synanon.

## **Founders and Notable Staff**

**Mel Wasserman**(1930-2002) was the founder of CEDU. He was also a devoted member of the Synanon cult.

**Brandi Elliot**worked as the Director of CEDU Middle School from 1998-2004. She later worked as the Admissions Director of [Northwest Academy](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/northwest) .

## **Program Structure**

CEDU Middle School's program was nearly identical to that of [CEDU High School](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/ceduhs) . CEDU Middle School utilized a level system consisting of 5 levels. The levels were:

***Discovery**
***Quest**
***Challenge**
***New Horizons**
***Summit**

CEDU Middle School was an extremely rigid program with many rules. Students at CEDU Middle School could not wear clothing with logos, listen to music, watch TV, use hair products, watch movies, etc.

The program at CEDU Middle School was very similar to the program at Synanon. Three times a week, for four hours at a time, students would attend "[Raps](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/ceduraps) ", which were pseudo-psychology group sessions led by untrained staff. "Raps" were heavily based on "The Game" at Synanon. Students and staff were encouraged to confront students for minor rule infractions in the name of emotional growth. Yelling was appropriate and expected.

In addition to "Raps", students at CEDU Middle also attended 20 - 24+ hour long workshops called "[Propheets](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/cedupropheets) ".

## **Abuse Allegations and Investigations**

CEDU Middle School has been widely recognized and confirmed as an abusive program. Instances of abuse and neglect which have been reported by survivors include extreme psychological abuse, sleep deprivation, physical/sexual abuse, solitary confinement, and forced manual labor. There are also reports that the staff were extremely unqualified and often violent individuals. Additionally, it has been reported that hundreds of teens attempted to escape for CEDU, some of which have never been seen again.
