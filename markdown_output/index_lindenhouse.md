# **Linden House**(1998-2000) St. George, UT

---

## **History and Background Information**

Linden House was a [WWASP](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/wwasp) behavior-modification program that opened sometime around 1998. It was founded by Willa Bundy, a former [Teen Help](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/teenhelp) /WWASP employee. The program was marketed as a specialty boarding school for pregnant teens and teenaged mothers.

The program was reportedly located at [30 E 750 S, St George, UT 84770](https://www.google.com/maps/place/30+E+750+S,+St.+George,+UT+84770/@37.0953488,-113.5827414,46m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x80ca4528f7974db7:0xacfe51eec73f7902!8m2!3d37.0953985!4d-113.5826383) .

The exact timespan that Linden House was in operation is not fully known. According to the program's [business license registration](https://imgur.com/a/l1gCs8K) , the program was registered on February 2^(nd) 1998. This license expired on October 16, 1998
