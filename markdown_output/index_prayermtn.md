# **Prayer Mountain Boys Academy**(2014-present) Meansville, GA

*Christian Boarding School*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Prayer Mountain Boys Academy is a [Teen Challenge](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/teenchallenge) behavior modification program that opened around 2014. It is marketed as a Christian Boarding School for teenage boys (12-17) who are struggling with life-impacting behaviors, rebellion, academic failure, or careless attitudes. They also enroll boys who are struggling with low self-esteem, rebellion,anger,defiance, deceitfulness, cheating, theft, manipulation, ADHD, Oppositional Defiance Disorder (ODD), Bipolar Disorder, Reactive Attachment Disorder (RAD), depression, promiscuity, self-harm or other risky behaviors, and not caring about their futures. The program's maximum enrollment is unknown, and the average length of stay is reported to be between 12 and 15 months. The cost of the program's tuition is roughly $4,000 per month.

The program is located at [736 US-19, Meansville, GA 30256](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Prayer+Mountain+Academy/@33.0023238,-84.3228421,836m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xe5dd0b0e8e43a5e6!8m2!3d33.0011586!4d-84.3246026) . The campus encompasses 36 acres and is situated in a rural part of Eastern Georgia, 50 miles south of Atlanta.
