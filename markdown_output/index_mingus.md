# **Mingus Mountain Academy**(1985-present) Prescott Valley, AZ

*Residential Treatment Center*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Mingus Mountain Academy is a [Sequel Youth and Family Services](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/sequelyouth) behavior-modification program that opened in January of 1985. It is marketed as a Residential Treatment Center for "emotionally and behaviorally at-risk" adolescent girls, aged 12-18. The program claims to be able to help treat teens who are struggling with Impulsive and irresponsible behaviors, Lacking motivation, no goals, and living for the moment, Denies or justifies negative behavior, Anger management and problems with aggression, Demonstrates a low degree of empathy, Lacks self-discipline, Poor coping skills, Substance abuse, thrill-seeking, and sexually promiscuous behaviors, Suicide ideations and self-abusive behaviors, Gang affiliations, Poor decision-making skills, Family problems-emotional damage by divorce, abuse, stepfamily situations, School problems, and Running away. Mingus Mountain Academy does not accept fire setters, pregnant girls, extremely physically violent youth, insulin dependent diabetics, and students with active psychotic features. The program has a maximum enrollment of 141 girls, and the average length of stay is unknown. Many girls who attend the academy have come from Juvenile Hall or past placements.

The program is located on a 120-acre campus at [15801 E Don Carlos Dr, Prescott Valley, AZ 86315](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Mingus+Mountain+Academy/@34.6429269,-112.1894049,561m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x872d23e34b7e1f33:0xc38b625a33889361!8m2!3d34.6430119!4d-112.1884958) .

** [Sequel Youth and Family Services](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/sequelyouth) is a confirmedly abusive organization that has allowed countless abuses and several deaths in their programs.**

## **Abuse Allegations and News**

Mingus Mountain Academy has been reported by many survivors to be an abusive program. Allegations of abuse that have been reported by survivors include sexual abuse, verbal abuse, and the use of violent physical restraints.

In May of 2020, Mingus Mountain Academy made headlines when it reported that 84 out of the 141 girls had tested positive for COVID-19. Prior to this outbreak, Yavapai County only had a total of 90 cases. This number soon increased to 113 total positive cases.

On July 20^(th) 2020, deputies were called to the Academy regarding a student striking a staff member in the head with a rock. Subsequently, several students instigated a disturbance requiring the involvement of numerous staff members before gaining control of the dorm. The student who assaulted the staff member was booked into the Juvenile Detention Center.

Only a day later, on July 21^(st) 2020, five girls were [arrested](https://www.azfamily.com/news/5-teens-facing-charges-after-attack-on-staff-at-all-girls-school-in-prescott-valley/article_60095a8e-cc5e-11ea-8b92-2746cc2dc63c.html) after allegedly attacking two staff members at Mingus Mountain Academy. According to what the staff told deputies, it started when a student who was not following directions got into a fight with a staff member. While the student was allegedly punching the staff member, two more girls joined in. When a second staff member stepped in to help, the original student grabbed a fire extinguisher and used it to hit the first staff member in the face, according to Yavapai County Sheriffs Office. The first staff suffered a possibly broken nose. The second staff member was also injured.
