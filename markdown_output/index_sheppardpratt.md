# **Sheppard Pratt at Ellicott City**(2002-present) Elliot City, MD

*Psychiatric Hospital*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Sheppard Pratt at Ellicott City (formerly known as [Taylor Manor Hospital](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/taylormanor) ) is a behavior-modification program that was rebranded in 2002. It is marketed as a private psychiatric hospital for children/teens and adults. The hospital currently has a 20-bed adult unit, an 18-bed co-occurring disorders unit, an 18-bed crisis stabilization unit, a 22-bed adolescent unit, and an adult day hospital. It is located at [4100 College Ave, Ellicott City, MD 21043](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Sheppard+Pratt+-+Ellicott+City+Campus/@39.2558504,-76.7948689,312m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x9d4da2ed08c8cefc!8m2!3d39.2557295!4d-76.7935238) , which is the same location previously used by Taylor Manor Hospital.

The hospital originally opened in 1907 under the name Patapsco Manor Sanitarium, which was built on a property along New Cut road in Ellicott City and was owned by Dr. Rushmore White.
