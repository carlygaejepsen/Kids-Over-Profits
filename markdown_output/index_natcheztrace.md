# **Natchez Trace Youth Academy**(2000-present) Waverly, TN

*Residential Treatment Center*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Natchez Trace Youth Academy is a behavior modification program that opened in 2000. It is marketed as a Residential Treatment Center for boys (10-17) who are struggling with a variety of emotional and behavioral issues. The program has a maximum enrollment of 115 boys, and the average length of stay is reported to be between 3 and 9 months. The cost of the program's tuition is presently unknown.

Natchez Trace Youth Academy is located at [415 7 Hawks Ln, Waverly, TN 37185](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Natchez+Trace+Youth+Academy/@35.8327489,-87.8711248,733m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xf07d5ede59177dd4!8m2!3d35.832112!4d-87.870434) .
