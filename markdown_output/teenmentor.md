# **Teen Mentor School**(2010-2011) Tarcoles, Costa Rica

*Behavior Modiciation Program*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Teen Mentor School was a [WWASP](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/wwasp) -affiliated behavior-modification program that was opened in October 2010 by the son of WWASP's founder, Robert W. Lichfield. It was marketed as a co-ed specialty boarding school for troubled teenagers (13-18).

The program was located in the converted [Carara hotel in Tarcoles, Costa Rica](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Carara+Hotel/@9.7605182,-84.6267771,294a,35y,270h/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xa9d14ec8240218ba!8m2!3d9.7609037!4d-84.6268565) .
