## **History and Background Information**

Adeona Healthcare (formerly known as Sovereign Health Group) is a company that owns/owned a chain of behavior-modification program that was founded in July 2014 under the name Sovereign Health. The programs were marketed as Rehabilitation programs for teenagers undergoing drug detoxification, as well as providing treatment for addiction and other mental health disorders, and were located throughout Southern California, Texas, Utah, Florida and Arizona. In 2017, the FBI raided several of the Southern California locations as part of an ongoing investigation, and the company closed these programs soon after. However, the program then changed its name and is currently marketing itself under the name Adeona Healthcare.

The programs are marketed as Residential Treatment Centers for teenagers () who are struggling with any of the following conditions: Addiction, PTSD, depression, anxiety, phobias, bullying, OCD, mood disorders, Schizoaffective disorder, Teen adjustment disorder, eating disorders, gaming addiction, cell phone addiction, social media addiction, etc. (and many more. These are all directly from their Current website. At the time I went “lgbt issues” was an option as well, it no longer is)
