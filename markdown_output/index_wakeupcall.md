# **Wake Up Call for Teens**(2017-2020) Donalds, SC

---

## **History and Background Information**

Wake Up Call for Teens was a [WWASP](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/wwasp) -affiliated Boot Camp/Boarding School which opened around 2017 in the location that was originally the campus of [Carolina Springs Academy](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/carolinasprings) . It was marketed as a "30 Day Intensive Behavioral-Fitness Boot Camp & Pioneer Trek Program" for teenagers aged 13-17. The tuition for this 30-day program was reported to be $6,995.

Wake Up Call for Teens was located at [205 Green Acres Ln, Donalds, SC 29638](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Palmetto+Therapeutic+Boarding+School/@34.3417919,-82.4070612,311a,35y,180h/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x5f244c4b7e636b2b!8m2!3d34.3414911!4d-82.4082574) . It was owned by and operated as a part of [Palmetto Boarding School](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/palmetto) , which shared the same campus.

The program appears to have closed around 2019 or 2020.

---

## **Founders and Notable Staff**

**Narvin Lichfield**is reported to have been affiliated or even owned Wake Up Call for Teens. He also reportedly has begun going by the name**Marvin Sheffield**. "Marvin" is listed as the admissions director of [Seneca Ranch](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/senecaranch) and Palmetto, as well as being the owner of [Carolina Springs Academy](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/carolinasprings) and the [Academy at Dundee Ranch](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/dundee) .

---

## **Program Structure**

According to their [website](https://web.archive.org/web/20170305121558/http://www.wakeupcallforteens.com/program.html) , the "short term 30 day process the Wake Up Call For Teens, is a Boot Camp Pioneer Trek Program that gives us the best of both worlds combining a Military Style Boot Camp proven to be effective in instilling discipline, honor and respect,and a Pioneer trek program that requires the students to trek daily up to 6-8 miles a day, setting up temporary tents for housing much like a wilderness program but with none of the environmental risks usually associated with such wilderness process's that typically operate in Desert or wilderness extreme environments."
