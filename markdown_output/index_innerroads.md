# **InnerRoads Wilderness Program**(2001-present) Missoula, MT

*Wilderness Program*

---

## **History and Background Information**

InnerRoads Wilderness Program is a behavior-modification program opened by Youth Homes Inc in 2001. It is marketed as a wilderness therapy program for teenagers (14-17) who are "looking to improve their self-esteem and to improve communication at home". From 2005 to 2017, it was operated by Youth Homes. In 2018, Inner Roads was established as an "independent non-profit" entity, after Youth Homes closed InnerRoads due to budget-cuts.

Because InnerRoads is a "wilderness" program, the students are not confined to a central campus but are instead taken on a 30-day hike around the area of the [Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selway%E2%80%93Bitterroot_Wilderness#:%7E:text=The%20Selway%E2%80%93Bitterroot%20Wilderness%20is,third%2Dlargest%20outside%20Alaska) and the [Helena National Forest](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Helena+National+Forest/@46.5499314,-112.2183509,3898m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x535ca9bb9d035f59:0x9ba75ff288bbbe99!8m2!3d46.5499332!4d-112.2008413) . The address associated with the program is [550 N California St, Missoula, MT 59802](https://www.google.com/maps/place/550+N+California+St,+Missoula,+MT+59802/@46.8766982,-114.0134872,143m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x535dce7bbda4ae6d:0x684da0f53e5ced78!8m2!3d46.8767941!4d-114.0130078) , but this is not the location of the program.

In January of 2018, Youth Homes was forced to close InnerRoads due to budget-cuts. However, the program has since reopened and is now being marketed as a seperate program that is unaffiliated with Youth Homes.

## ** Program Structure**

The program consist of two components:

*A 30-day wilderness hike which ends with a solo-task where the teenagers are out in the wilderness alone.
*A community component where the youth will work around a place called the [Randolph Homestead](https://www.google.com/maps/place/1515+Spurlock+Rd,+Missoula,+MT+59802/@46.9005589,-113.9999954,884m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x535dcebff839be8f:0xdf9a0be89881b6ed!8m2!3d46.900062!4d-114.0018872?hl=en) . This part last about 2 weeks. While working there, the teenager is being teamed up with a mentor who will follow the teenager as an after-care program for one year.
