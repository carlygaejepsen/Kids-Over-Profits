# **Adolescent Guidance Services, LLC**(1996-present) San Ramon, CA

---

## ** History and Background Information**

Adolescent Guidance Services, LLC is a teen transport company founded in 1996. In addition to transportation of teenagers to troubled teen facilities, the company also offers security/personal protection services, location services of runaway children/teens, anger management services, crisis intervention, conflict resolution, and afterschool programs for children.

Adolescent Guidance Services' main office is located at [231 Market Pl #194, San Ramon, CA 94583](https://www.google.com/maps/place/231+Market+Pl+%23194,+San+Ramon,+CA+94583/@37.7638609,-121.9525535,93m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x808ff28182fda679:0x1669cc4f21008917!8m2!3d37.7639129!4d-121.952434) . They focus their transportation services on teenagers located in the San Francisco Bay Area and surrounding communities. AGS claims that it transports teenagers across the country and also internationally. The company also has an office in Southern California, at [2706 Harbor Blvd #209a, Costa Mesa, CA 92626](https://www.google.com/maps/place/2706+Harbor+Blvd+%23209a,+Costa+Mesa,+CA+92626/@33.6710702,-117.9186903,158m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x80dcdf66b1030961:0x35939ef72550e287!8m2!3d33.6710825!4d-117.918546) .

## ** Founders and Notable Staff**

** William Lopez** is the Founder and CEO of Adolescent Guidance Services. He received an Associates Degree in Criminal Justice and Corrections from Foothill College in 1986, and then went on to receive a Bachelor's degree in Criminology from San Diego State University in 1989. He also earned a Bachelor's degree in Sociology from San Jose State University in 1993. He began his career working as an Institution Services Manager at the San Mateo County Probation office in 1987. He then worked as a Senior Investigator and Partner Owner at Gladding & Michel Investigations from 1993 until 2006. This company reportedly investigated Product Liabilty, Fatal Accidents, Wrongful Termination Cases, and Fraud-related legal cases. During his employment as an investigator, he created AGS in 1996. According to AGS's [archived website](https://web.archive.org/web/20000520011901/https://www.escort-transport.com/) , AGS actually began as a division of Gladding & Michel Investigations. Since the company's creation, Lopez claims to have supervised "over 26,880 incident free transports of at risk youth." He also currently works as the CEO of LW VINEYARDS AND OLIVE OIL, LLC, beginning in 2019.

** Gabriela Lopez** is the Operations Manager of Adolescent Guidance Services. She received an Associates degree in Liberal Arts and Sciences/Liberal Studies from Diablo Valley College in an unnknown year. She then received a Bachelor's degree for the same subject from CSU Long Beach in 2021. She began her career as a Child Care Specialist at Little Bridges Child Care Center from 2015 until 2018. She then began working at AGS in June 2019. She also currently works as a Team Supervisor Supported Choices, Inc. in San Diego, CA.

** Horace L. Miller** worked as the Director and President of Adolescent Guidance Services until 2006. In this position, he was in charge of the day to day operations of AGS, including investigating runaways, crisis intervention, staff training, and supervision of transport agents. According to his [staff bio](https://web.archive.org/web/20020607055541/http://www.escort-transport.com/bios.html) , Horace had been transporting teenagers since 1988- eight years before AGS was created. He had also previously served as a paratrooper in the United States Army. He currently appears to work as the President of [Journeys Adolescent Services, LLC](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/journeystransport) .

** Danny Ray Carley** worked as a Supervisor and Transport Agent at Adolescent Guidance Services until roughly 2005. Like Horace Miller, he previously worked [Journeys Adolescent Services, LLC](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/journeystransport) before joining AGS. He reportedly has "extensive experience" working with residential treatment facilities and juvenile probation departments. His current employment is presently unknown.

** Tina Vitale** worked as the Senior Director of Adolescent Guidance Services until roughly 2013. She, too, previously worked at [Journeys Adolescent Services, LLC](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/journeystransport) . She currently works as a Transport Agent at [Right Direction Crisis Intervention](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/rightdirection) .

## ** Program/TTI Affiliations**

Adolescent Guidance Services has been known to transport teens to many reportedly and confirmedly abusive TTI programs, including but not limited to:

* [Diamond Ranch Academy](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/diamondranch)

It is known that the Educational Consultant Firm, [The Bodin Group](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/bodingroup) , frequently recommends its clients to employ the use of Adolescent Guidance Services to transport their children to their selected TTI program.
