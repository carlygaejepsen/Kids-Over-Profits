# **Cookson Hills Christian School**(1957-present) Kansas, OK

*Christian Therapeutic Boarding School*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Cookson Hills Christian School is a behavior modification program that opened in 1957. It is marketed as a Christian Therapeutic Boarding School/Group Home for children and teens (5-17) who are struggling with issues such as challenges with family life, homelessness, poor peer relationships, abuse, or neglect. The program has a maximum enrollment of 120 residents, and the average length of stay is presently unknown, but is often much longer than 2-3 years. The cost of the program's tuition is presently unknown.

Cookson Hills Christian School is located at [60416 OK-10, Kansas, OK 74347](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Cookson+Hills+Christian+School/@36.1412707,-94.7886894,954m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x7b6d1d69b521eb8d!8m2!3d36.13981!4d-94.7851995) . The campus is spread across 1,000 acres in a rural part of northeastern Oklahoma.
