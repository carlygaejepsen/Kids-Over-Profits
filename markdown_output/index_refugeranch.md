# **The Refuge Ranch**(2018-present) Bastrop, TX

---

## **History and Background Information**

The Refuge Ranch is a residential program that opened in 2018. It is marketed as a Residential Treatment Center for girls (14-19) who have been a victim of sex trafficking.
