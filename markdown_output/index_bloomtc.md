# **Bloom, A Place for Girls**(2017-present) Brockton, MA

*Residential Treatment Center*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Bloom, A Place for Girls (also called Bloom Home for Girls or Bloom Teen Challenge) is a [Teen Challenge](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/teenchallenge) behavior-modification program that opened in January 2017. It is marketed as a short-term residential treatment center for teenage girls (12-17) who are struggling with issues such as substance abuse, depression, self-harm, behavioral problems, anger, running away, rebelliousness, and more. The average length of stay is between 3 and 5 months, and the maximum enrollment is 10 girls. The cost of the program is unknown.

The program is located at [1315 Main St, Brockton, MA 02301](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Bloom+A+Place+for+Girls/@42.0592549,-71.0147686,78m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x1beb049b25820f0a!8m2!3d42.059215!4d-71.0143421) .
