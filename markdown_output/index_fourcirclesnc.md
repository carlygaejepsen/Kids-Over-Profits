# **Four Circles Recovery Center**(2006-2018) Horse Shoe, NC

*Residential Treatment Center*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Four Circles Recovery Center was an [Aspen Education Group](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/aspeneducation) behavior-modification program that opened in 2006. It was marketed as a Residential Treatment Center for young adults (18-28) who were struggling with addiction, substance abuse, and co-occuring mental health disorders.

The program was located at [156 Clear Crossing Ln, Horse Shoe, NC 28742](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Four+Circles+Recovery+Center/@35.3988203,-82.6126938,295m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xfe886967e2678df2!8m2!3d35.398876!4d-82.611418) .

Unlike other Aspen Education Group programs, Four Circles only enrolled young adults who voluntarily signed themselves in to the program. As such, they were allowed to leave at any time.

---

## **Founders and Notable Staff**

**Jack Kline**worked as the Executive Director of Four Circles from 2006 until 2014. Prior to this, he worked at the reportedly abusive [SUWS of the Carolinas](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/suwsnc) from around 2002 until 2005, and at [Phoenix Outdoor](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/phoenixoutdoor) from 2005-2006, which are both owned by [Aspen Education Group](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/aspeneducation) . Currently, he works for Red Oak Recovery, which is another substance abuse program for young adults.
