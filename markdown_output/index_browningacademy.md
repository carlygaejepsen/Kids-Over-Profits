# **Browning Academy**(1996-1999)

---

## **History and Background Information**

Browning Academy was the name of the WWASP company that provided the "education" services to the teens enrolled in WWASP programs.

The "school" was "accredited" by the [Northwest Accreditation Commission (NWAC)](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/nwac) . However, it is important to note that neither the United States Department of Education nor the Council for Higher Education Accreditation recognizes the Northwest Accreditation Commission as an accrediting agency for institutions of higher education.

The business' registration states that the registered agent is Ralph J. Atkin, a longtime trustee of WWASP and owner of Skywest Airlines, Eurosky Airlines, and Jet Aquisitions Group. The address associated with the company is [50 E 100 S No. 205, St George, UT 84770](https://www.google.com/maps/place/50+E+100+S+%23205,+St.+George,+UT+84770/@37.1062181,-113.5823653,86m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x80ca44d0524ab57d:0x6c25302b1122569e!8m2!3d37.1061753!4d-113.5820018) , which is an address used by many WWASP companies.
