# **Red Rock Academy**(1999-2000) Washington County, UT

*Specialty Boarding School*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Red Rock Academy was a [WWASP](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/wwasp) behavior-modification program that appears to have opened around 1999. It was the predecessor for WWASP's [Cross Creek Center](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/crosscreek) for boys, which opened shortly after.

The program was located on a 120-acre campus [off of Utah SR-59, near Zion National Park](https://www.google.com/maps/place/37%C2%B007'41.4%22N+113%C2%B015'18.5%22W/@37.128177,-113.2573227,569m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m14!1m7!3m6!1s0x80cae45f3f37b3df:0x81fd599aafd3135d!2sUT-59,+Utah!3b1!8m2!3d37.1026054!4d-113.1328305!3m5!1s0x0:0x0!7e2!8m2!3d37.1281766!4d-113.2551335) . This appears to be the same location that was used by [Red Rock Springs](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/redrocksprings) , another WWASP program that was apparently shorter-term.

The exact timespan during whih Red Rock Academy operated is not fully known. According to the program's [business registration](https://imgur.com/a/B60FrT9) , RRA was created on March 24, 1999. This license expired on April 17, 2002 due to failure to renew it, but the program was licensed again on March 28, 2003.
