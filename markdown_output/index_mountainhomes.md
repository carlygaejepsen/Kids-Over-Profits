# **Mountain Homes Youth Ranch**(1996-2014) Vernal, UT/Dinosaur, CO

*Wilderness Program*

---

## **History and Background Information**

Mountain Homes Youth Ranch was a behavior-modification program founded in 1996. It was marketed as an Outdoor Therapeutic Program/Wilderness Program for troubled teenagers aged 12-18. The program enrolls teenagers with a history of ADD/ADHD, ODD/OCD, peer pressure, low self-esteem, runaway history, anger management, anxiety, substance abuse, lack of motivation, school related problems, respect issues, and defiance. The average length of stay was reportedly between 12 and 15 weeks. MHYR was a member of [NATSAP](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/natsap) and had been since 2005. It was also a member of the [Outdoor Behavioral Healthcare Industry Council (OBHIC)](https://www.reddit.com/r/troubledteens/wiki/index/obhic) .

The address associated with the program is listed as [247 S. Vernal Ave, Vernal, UT 84078](https://www.google.com/maps/place/247+S+Vernal+Ave,+Vernal,+UT+84078/@40.452268,-109.5283619,92m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x874562497611ba1d:0x3f065b14b7e1b2e3!8m2!3d40.4522256!4d-109.5281025) . However, it is reported that the program was actually located on a 15,000 acre ranch near the remote town of [Dinosaur, Colorado](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Dinosaur,+CO+81610/@40.2400493,-109.0122232,2424m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x8745a5fae709b0e9:0x2a9ade53888285be!8m2!3d40.243578!4d-109.0145613) .

Mountain Homes Youth Ranch also operated a wilderness program for young adults (18-25) called Ashley Valley Wilderness. Ashley Valley Wilderness program still appears to be in operation.
