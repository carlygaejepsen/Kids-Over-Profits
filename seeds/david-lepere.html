Research draft - not for publication. This profile relies on a 2007 Cherokee Creek press release and the KOP network map. Program histories (CEDU's methods, its 2005 bankruptcy) are attributed to the programs, not to LePere personally.

LePere directed both CEDU High School and CEDU Middle School, then ran Wilderness Quest and Cherokee Creek Boys School. The KOP network map also lists him as a NATSAP board president. He carries the CEDU lineage, which descends from Synanon, into the post-CEDU industry and its trade association. The map shows seven links across four networks.1

Roles and timeline

  • About 1992 onward: Fifteen years in the field by 2007, including Mount Bachelor Academy (Oregon).1
  • Before 2005: Director of CEDU High School and CEDU Middle School (Running Springs, California).1
  • Before 2007: Executive director of Wilderness Quest (Monticello, Utah), billed as the country's longest-running wilderness therapy program.1
  • November 2007: Executive director of Cherokee Creek Boys School (Westminster, South Carolina), a NATSAP school for middle-school boys.1
  • Undated: NATSAP board president, and board member of Compass Rose Academy, according to the network map.

Programs and their histories

  • CEDU High School and Middle School: CEDU, founded by Mel Wasserman on Synanon's model, used confrontational "raps" and emotional "propheet" workshops. The CEDU schools closed in 2005 when the company went bankrupt.
  • Wilderness Quest: Founded by Larry Wells, who said he had run wilderness programs for troubled teens since 1971.
  • Cherokee Creek Boys School: Founded in 2003 by Beth and Ron Black for boys with ADHD, autism, anxiety and learning disabilities. The network map files it in the Aspiro frame.

Connections

  • Spouse: Janine Riehl LePere, linked on the network map to Cherokee Creek and Mount Bachelor Academy.
  • CEDU lineage: Mel Wasserman and Joe Ricci (the map's Brown Schools/CEDU frame).
  • NATSAP leadership: Kimball DeLaMare, Jan Moss, Rosemary Tippett and the other presidents profiled on this site.
  • Network map: CEDU High School and CEDU Middle School (director), Wilderness Quest and Cherokee Creek (executive director), NATSAP (board president) and Compass Rose Academy (board).
