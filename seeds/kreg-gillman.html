Research draft - not for publication. This profile relies mainly on Gillman's own 2004 Woodbury Reports interview, which ran as a paid placement, and on Breaking Code Silence. Program histories are attributed to the programs, not to him.

Gillman is a therapist turned executive who links more networks on the KOP network map than anyone else: seven programs across six frames. He ran Aspen Ranch and SunHawk Academy for Aspen Education Group, then Provo Canyon School for Universal Health Services, and co-founded Silverado with Provo Canyon's co-founder. He shows how the same leadership circulated between Aspen, UHS and independent Utah programs.1, 2

Roles and timeline

  • 1993: Therapist at Provo Canyon School.1
  • 1996: Director of admissions at Provo Canyon School.1
  • 1998: Director of admissions for Aspen Achievement Academy and Aspen Ranch (Aspen Education Group).1
  • About 1998-2001: Executive director of Aspen Ranch for three years.1
  • About 2001-2003: Executive director of SunHawk Academy (St. George, Utah), at Aspen's request.1
  • January 2004: Returned to Provo Canyon School as CEO, under UHS.1
  • About 2006: Helped Provo Canyon co-founder Robert Crist start Silverado; the network map lists him as executive director of Silverado Boys Ranch.2
  • Later: CEO of Salt Lake Behavioral Health (UHS).2

The network map also links him to RedCliff Ascent (clinical director) and WayPoint Academy (executive director). Neither appears in the sources found so far.

Programs he led and what happened to them

  • Provo Canyon School: Abuse and overmedication allegations stretch back to its 1971 founding. Utah revoked the license for its main Provo campus on July 17, 2026, and UHS closed the school. His CEO years were 2004 to about 2006.3
  • Silverado: Closed in 2016 after reported sexual abuse, according to Breaking Code Silence.2
  • Aspen Ranch: An Aspen program in Loa, Utah, run on a six-level system like Provo Canyon's. It later closed.

Connections

  • Robert Crist: Provo Canyon co-founder and Silverado partner.2
  • Ken Huey and Brad Gerrard: Gillman visited Woodbury Reports with them in fall 2003.1
  • Industry media: His 2004 Woodbury Reports profile ran as a paid advertisement.1
  • Network map: Provo Canyon School and UHS (CEO); Aspen Ranch, SunHawk Academy, WayPoint Academy and Silverado Boys Ranch (executive director); RedCliff Ascent (clinical director).
