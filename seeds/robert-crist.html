Crist is a psychiatrist who co-founded Provo Canyon School in 1971 with Jack Williams and served as its medical director. Survivors describe heavy medication under his care. A federal court enjoined him and the school over their treatment of residents in 1979. He went on to co-found or serve as medical director at Discovery Academy, Logan River Academy and Silverado. Provo Canyon, which Utah shut down in 2026, is often called the birthplace of WWASPS, since Robert Lichfield and Karr Farnsworth started there.1, 2, 3

Roles and timeline

  • 1971: Co-founded Provo Canyon School (boys only at first) with Jack Williams; owner and medical director.1, 4
  • 1979: A federal court permanently enjoined Provo Canyon School and Crist over residents' treatment, in a suit against owners Williams and Crist.2
  • 1986: Provo Canyon sold to Charter Medical Corporation.4
  • 1989: Co-founded Discovery Academy with former Provo Canyon co-owner Eugene Thorne and Brent Hall; the network map lists him as owner and vice president.2
  • 2000: Provo Canyon sold to UHS. Crist became medical director of Logan River Academy, founded by Provo Canyon alumni Larry Carter and Jeff Smith.3
  • About 2006: Co-founded Silverado with former Provo Canyon CEO Kreg Gillman; it closed in 2016 after reported sexual abuse.2

The 1979 injunction

Former residents sued owners Williams and Crist, challenging Provo Canyon's education, treatment and confinement methods. The resulting permanent injunction restricted practices at the school. It is one of the earliest court orders against a troubled teen program.2

Medication

As medical director, Crist was directly responsible for medicating residents. Many Provo Canyon survivors say they were overmedicated. One account collected by Breaking Code Silence describes begging him to stop medications, after which he prescribed more.2

The Provo Canyon family tree

  • WWASPS: Robert Lichfield, Karr Farnsworth and Brent Facer worked at Provo Canyon before opening Cross Creek Manor in 1987-1988, the first WWASPS program.
  • Discovery Academy: Crist, Eugene Thorne and Brent Hall. Thorne previously ran BYU's psychology department, where aversion therapy was used on gay students.2
  • Logan River Academy: Larry Carter (13 years at Provo Canyon), Jeff Smith (former Provo Canyon CEO), with Crist as medical director. In 2019 a former resident sued, alleging repeated sexual abuse by an employee.3
  • Silverado: Crist and Kreg Gillman.2

Provo Canyon itself closed in August 2026 after Utah revoked its license.1
