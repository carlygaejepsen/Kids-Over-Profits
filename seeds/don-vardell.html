Vardell is a career program executive who has run at least six programs in five states, most of them for Aspen Education Group. He came from hospital marketing rather than clinical work. He is a good example of the professional administrator that chains move from program to program. Several of his programs were later sold or closed.1, 2, 3

Roles and timeline

  • Before 2004: Senior marketing and business development officer at a tertiary care hospital in Northern Virginia.1
  • Before 2004: Administrator of an adolescent residential treatment center in Tennessee, identified by Jared Balmer as Peninsula Village.1, 3
  • 2004-early 2007: Executive director, Academy at Swift River (Cummington, Massachusetts; Aspen).2
  • August 2007: Executive director, Excel Academy (Conroe, Texas; Aspen).2, 4
  • December 2008: Executive director, Island View RTC, replacing founder Jared Balmer.3
  • Later: Executive director of Shortridge Academy and Mountain Valley Treatment Center (New Hampshire), and of the Aspen Institute for Behavioral Assessment, according to the network map.

Programs and what happened to them

  • Academy at Swift River: Aspen closed it in 2013.
  • Island View: Sold in 2014 and rebranded as Elevations RTC, now a Family Help & Wellness-affiliated program.
  • Aspen Institute for Behavioral Assessment: Became Viewpoint Center under Family Help & Wellness.
  • Excel Academy and Shortridge Academy: The network map marks both closed.

Connections

  • Aspen Education Group: Bo Turner, senior vice president of residential programs, who rehired him in 2007.2
  • Island View founders: Jared Balmer, Kimball DeLaMare and Lorin Broadbent.
  • Network map: Six programs across the Wilderness, Altior, Eckerd and Aspen frames, making him one of the map's most cross-network people.
